Education

Experience

AI Developer and Research Design Intern — Peraton

Reston, VA (Remote) · Aug 2026 – present

Cyber Operations, Cyber and Intelligence Sector; mentor Ebenezer Dadson. Building software powered by large language models and multi-agent systems, and designing evaluation protocols and controlled experiments measuring how design parameters and system factors affect performance and reliability.

Graduate Student Researcher — Baylor College of Medicine

Houston, TX · 2022 – present

Liu Lab, Data Science Center, Duncan Neurological Research Institute, Texas Children’s Hospital; advisor Dr. Zhandong Liu.

  • ClinPreAI — agentic AI system (research, planning, coding, debugging, and interpretation modules) for early postpartum depression risk prediction (EPDS ≥ 10, 31% prevalence) from a single antepartum hospitalization; 4,161 patients, 27 structured variables plus social worker notes; F1 0.68 structured / 0.65 multimodal, matching a custom LLM-XGBoost pipeline.
  • Pediatric diabetes (T1D/T2D) — PySpark pipeline on Palantir Foundry built from OMOP CDM and Epic Clarity EHR data; T2D n = 2,489, T1D n = 4,018; seven binary outcomes at 2- and 5-year horizons; multi-agent LLM clinical-reasoning architectures compared against eight classical ML and deep-learning baselines.
  • Trofinetide / Rett syndrome — dual-LLM consensus pipeline extracting 18 treatment-response phenotypes from 26,763 notes (108 patients); 84.2% accuracy and F1 0.674 on the expert-annotated development set.
  • Clinical-note de-identification — benchmarked eight LLMs against Stanford TiDE, OpenMed PII, and pattern baselines on 100 expert-annotated pediatric oncology notes (5,322 PHI spans); best single-pass F1 0.918 ± 0.001; introduced “institutionally-situated PHI”; used LLM outputs to surface 414 candidate gold-standard annotation gaps.
  • TEDDY — contributed to a 1.84M-parameter generative pediatric EHR foundation model trained on ~1.6M children and ~73M ICD-10 codes; median first-occurrence AUC 0.72 across 797 disease-onset tasks.

Research Associate — NASA Ames Research Center

Mountain View, CA · May – Aug 2022 (collaboration continued remotely through Aug 2026)

Space Biosciences Division; mentor Dr. Jessica Lee. Developed the AMMPER model simulating cell damage under space radiation, validated against BioSentinel mission data; added gamma radiation and alamarBlue metabolic-dye dynamics (Michaelis-Menten kinetics) and a 3D free-radical diffusion model; mentored two undergraduates who carried the project forward; four presentations.

Machine Learning Researcher Intern — NASA Johnson Space Center

Houston, TX · 2021 – 2022

Mentor Terry Hill. Built an NLP pipeline over historical engineering failure reports and an LDA-BERT taxonomy for space-hardware failure reporting; delivered Power BI dashboards and interactive knowledge graphs (INDRA-EIDOS, spaCy, Bokeh-networkx); identified 5–15 critical engineering processes; eight presentations.

Undergraduate Researcher — University of Houston

Houston, TX · 2020 – 2021

Advisor Dr. Pavan Hosur. Theoretical work on asymmetric superconductivity under broken symmetries; modeled asymmetric supercurrents in non-centrosymmetric superconductors; one conference presentation; one Physical Review B publication.
